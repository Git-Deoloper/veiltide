# Community conduct

Be respectful, inclusive, and constructive. Help others reproduce bugs safely, give specific code-focused feedback, and credit contributions. Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, doxxing, abusive comments and spam are not tolerated. Maintainers may moderate or remove disruptive content.

Use GitHub's platform abuse-reporting tools for serious conduct issues. For sensitive security concerns, refer to `SECURITY.md`; never post credentials or unredacted logs in public discussions.
